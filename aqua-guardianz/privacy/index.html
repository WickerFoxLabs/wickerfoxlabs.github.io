Privacy Policy

Aqua Guardianz, by Wicker Fox Labs · Effective date: October 23, 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Aqua Guardianz ("the game") handles. In short: the game does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal information.

Information we collect

None. The game does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts or any other personal information, and it does not include advertising or analytics services.

Your game progress

Your progress (your fish, tanks, coins, pearls and settings) is saved only on your device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the game schedules reminders on your device (for example, when your fish have grown or a daily reward is ready). They are created on your device and not sent from any server. You can turn them off in the game's settings or in your phone's settings at any time.

In-app purchases

The game may offer optional in-app purchases. Payments are handled entirely by Google Play under Google's Privacy Policy; we never see or store your payment details.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If the game adds features that handle information (such as online leaderboards or cloud saves), this policy will be updated before those features are released, and the effective date above will change.
