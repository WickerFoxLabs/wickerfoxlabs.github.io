Privacy Policy

Aqua Guardianz, by Wicker Fox Labs · Effective date: October 24, 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Aqua Guardianz ("the game") handles. In short: the game uses Google Play Games for leaderboards and cloud saves and Google Firebase to understand how the game is played and to fix crashes. It has no advertising, and we never sell your information.

Information the game uses

The game does not ask for your name, email, location or contacts. It uses only what Google Play Games provides when you are signed in:

  • Your Play Games player ID and gamer name, to show you on leaderboards.
  • Your scores (such as your level and the fish you have raised), sent to Google Play Games leaderboards.
  • Your saved game, backed up with Google Play Games so you can continue on another device.

This information is stored and processed by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. We do not receive your email, contacts or other Google account details, and we do not sell or share any information with third parties. Signing in to Play Games is optional: the game works fully without it, and progress then stays only on your device.

Analytics and crash reports (Google Firebase)

To improve the game and fix problems, the game uses Google Firebase:

  • Firebase Analytics records how the game is used, for example levels reached, fish species raised, packs opened, events joined and purchases made, together with device information (model, operating system, language, approximate country) and an app-instance identifier.
  • Firebase Crashlytics sends a report if the game crashes: the device model and operating system, the state of the game at the time (such as your level and current tank) and technical details of the error.
  • Firebase Remote Config lets us adjust game settings (such as how often the free pearl chest appears) without an update; it uses the same app-instance identifier.

This data is processed by Google on our behalf under the Firebase privacy and security terms. It is not used for advertising, it does not include your name, email or contacts, and we do not sell it or share it with other companies.

Your game progress

Your progress (your fish, tanks, coins, pearls and settings) is saved on your device and, if you are signed in to Play Games, backed up to your Google Play Games account. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data removes the copy on your device.

Deleting your data

You can delete the game's cloud save and leaderboard entries from the Google Play Games app (Profile and settings) or from your Google Account at myaccount.google.com. Analytics data is kept by Firebase for a limited time and is linked only to an app-instance identifier; uninstalling the game resets it. You can also email us (below) to ask for any data linked to you to be deleted, and we will help.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the game schedules reminders on your device (for example, when your fish have grown or a daily reward is ready); these are created on your device. We may also send occasional news (such as a new event) as push messages through Firebase Cloud Messaging, which uses a device token for delivery. You can turn off reminders and news separately in the game's notification settings, or all notifications in your phone's settings, at any time.

In-app purchases

The game may offer optional in-app purchases. Payments are handled entirely by Google Play under Google's Privacy Policy; we never see or store your payment details.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If the game adds features that handle other information, this policy will be updated before those features are released, and the effective date above will change.
